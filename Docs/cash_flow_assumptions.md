# Amazon Cash Flow Assumptions

## 2025 Historical Baseline

All financial amounts are in USD millions.

- Operating cash flow: 139,514
- Purchases of property and equipment: 131,819
- Operating cash flow less property and equipment purchases: 7,695
- Reported depreciation, amortization, and other: 65,756
- Stock-based compensation: 19,467

Source: Amazon 2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows.

## Modeling Considerations

1. The reported depreciation, amortization, and other line includes items beyond pure depreciation and amortization. It must not be treated as a clean depreciation expense without further reconciliation.

2. Capital expenditures are particularly important because Amazon is investing heavily in AWS infrastructure.

3. Operating cash flow is not the same as unlevered free cash flow (FCFF). FCFF must be calculated using operating profit after taxes, appropriate noncash adjustments, capital expenditures, and changes in operating working capital.

4. Historical cash flow does not establish the correct future capital expenditure rate. Forecast assumptions require separate evidence and sensitivity testing.

5. Stock-based compensation is a noncash operating cash flow adjustment, but the valuation must also account consistently for its economic cost and dilution.

## Items Requiring Further Research

- Depreciation and amortization excluding unrelated adjustments
- Capital expenditure guidance and infrastructure investment
- Operating working capital and its historical changes
- Debt, financing costs, and lease obligations
- Cash flow sensitivity to AWS growth and investment spending

## Cash Flow Forecast Driver Framework

### Capital expenditures
- 2025 property and equipment purchases: $131,819 million.
- 2025 revenue: $716,924 million.
- Historical purchases/revenue ratio: approximately 18.39%.
- Forecast driver: Annual capital expenditures as a percentage of revenue.
- Challenge: AWS expansion and AI infrastructure spending may cause future investment intensity to differ substantially from 2025.
- Status: Forecast percentage not yet selected.

### Depreciation and amortization
- 2025 reported depreciation, amortization, and other: $65,756 million.
- This includes capitalized content costs, operating lease assets, and other items.
- Forecast driver: Depreciation and amortization based on existing assets and future investment.
- Challenge: Useful lives and capital expenditure timing affect depreciation expense.
- Status: Clean historical D&A and forecast schedule still required.

### Operating net working capital
- 2025 cash flow adjustments from changes in operating assets and liabilities: -$19,969 million.
- These adjustments are not equivalent to a clean operating NWC balance or its change.
- Forecast driver: Operating current assets minus operating current liabilities, using consistent classifications.
- Challenge: Amazon's negative working capital and changes in supplier payment timing could materially affect future cash flow.
- Status: Historical balance sheet accounts and forecast assumptions still required.

### FCFF calculation
FCFF = EBIT × (1 - cash tax assumption) + D&A - CapEx - Change in operating NWC.

Cash taxes, accounting taxes, depreciation, stock-based compensation, leases, and financing items must be handled consistently.

No final valuation should rely on placeholder cash flow assumptions without documented evidence and sensitivity testing.

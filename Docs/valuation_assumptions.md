### Equity Risk Premium

- Valuation date: September 1, 2026
- Implied U.S. equity risk premium: 4.14%
- Method: Trailing 12-month adjusted payout
- Source: Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern
- URL: https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/home.htm
- Model input: 0.0414
- Status: Dated published market estimate

Preliminary CAPM specification:
Cost of equity = 4.79% + Beta × 4.14%

Amazon's equity beta still requires a documented estimate
using data available by the valuation date.

Damodaran's published ERP estimate used a 4.75%
Treasury-rate convention. Our separately sourced Treasury
yield is 4.79%; this difference must be disclosed.

## Terminal Value

Method: Perpetuity-growth model.

Terminal value = FCFF in 2031 / (WACC - terminal growth).

Requirements:
- Terminal growth must be less than WACC.
- Long-term reinvestment assumptions must be economically sustainable.
- Sensitivity testing must cover alternative terminal growth rates.

Status: Terminal growth assumption not yet finalized.

# Amazon — Assumption Challenge Record

## Driver 1: Consolidated Revenue Growth (2026)

**Value:** 12.0% (preliminary base-case assumption)

**Basis:** Amazon's 2024 revenue growth was 10.99% and its 2025 revenue growth was 12.38%, calculated from the 2025 Form 10-K. The 12% assumption is close to recent historical performance.

**Challenge:** Historical consolidated growth may not represent future segment-level growth. AWS demand, retail competition, infrastructure constraints, and economic conditions could cause actual revenue growth to differ.

**Evidence that would change it:** Amazon's quarterly 2026 reports and guidance available by September 1, 2026; AWS, North America, and International segment results; and evidence of changing customer demand.

**Status:** Preliminary. Requires segment-level research, a defensible sensitivity range, and comparison with information available by the valuation date.

**Source:** Amazon.com, Inc., 2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Operations, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm

**Next test:** Calculate forecast revenue from the 2025 base year, verify the arithmetic, then test whether changing the growth assumption changes revenue in the expected direction.

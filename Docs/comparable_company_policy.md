# Amazon Comparable-Company Valuation Policy

## Purpose
Use market multiples to provide an independent
valuation reference alongside the FCFF DCF model.

## Candidate Reference Companies

### Walmart (WMT)
- Relevant business exposure: large-scale retail.
- Limitation: does not have Amazon's AWS cloud business.

### Microsoft (MSFT)
- Relevant business exposure: enterprise cloud computing.
- Limitation: software economics and business mix differ
  substantially from Amazon's consolidated operations.

### Alphabet (GOOGL)
- Relevant business exposure: digital advertising and cloud.
- Limitation: search advertising and other activities have
  different economics from Amazon's retail operations.

## Valuation Methods to Research
- Enterprise value / revenue
- Enterprise value / EBITDA
- Price / earnings, where appropriate

## Comparison Rules
- Use market prices available by September 1, 2026.
- Match enterprise values with appropriate financial metrics.
- Use consistent trailing or forward financial periods.
- Explain differences in margins, growth, reinvestment,
  capital intensity, and business mix.
- Do not treat a peer median as a definitive Amazon value.

## Remaining Work
- Gather dated market capitalization and debt/cash data.
- Gather comparable financial metrics.
- Calculate multiples using consistent conventions.
- Apply and justify an appropriate valuation range.
- Reconcile the peer-based valuation with the DCF.
